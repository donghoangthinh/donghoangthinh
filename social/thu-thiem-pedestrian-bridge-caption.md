Saigon is getting its first walking bridge across the river.

The main steel arch of the Thu Thiem pedestrian bridge is now in place. The design is based on the nipa palm leaf (lá dừa nước), a plant of the Mekong Delta. The bridge links Bach Dang Wharf in Saigon Ward with the Thu Thiem riverside park.

Ground notes for partners planning Saigon programs:
• Length: approx. 720 m, deck 6–11 m wide, pedestrians only
• Route: Nguyen Hue walking street → Bach Dang Wharf → across the river → Thu Thiem, with views of Landmark 81 and Ba Son Bridge
• Program fit: evening city walks, incentive group photo moments, a river-to-skyline segment between dinner and a Saigon River cruise
• Status: still under construction, not yet open to the public. We will share access hours, group-size rules and the opening date once they are officially confirmed.

Our ops team is already planning group movements around the bridge so it can go into your Saigon itineraries as soon as it opens.
